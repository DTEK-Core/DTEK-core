# Obsidian Control Center Setup

## Objective

Создать единый локальный центр управления DTEK без переноса проекта во вторую
систему и без дублирования sources of truth.

## Delivered

- Executive dashboard and current-state map.
- Product/startup map and Mermaid diagrams.
- Roadmap milestones and all Sprint 01–35 catalog.
- Development, Ideas, QA and Risk Kanban boards.
- Decision, Metrics and Pilot registers.
- Design/Figma workflow and Go-To-Market/pilot map.
- Reporting hub and reusable templates.
- Obsidian setup and shared owner/Codex operating model.

## Boundaries

- Production code, schema, migrations and RLS were not changed.
- Security Gate was not started or marked complete.
- Historical Sprint content was not duplicated or rewritten.
- Community plugins are recommended but not installed automatically.
